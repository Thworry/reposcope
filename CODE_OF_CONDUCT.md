# Code of Conduct

RepoScope contributors, maintainers, and community participants are expected to make participation respectful, safe, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, languages, abilities, and levels of experience.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Protect people's privacy and follow the private security-reporting process.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, stalking, or discriminatory remarks.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, insults, or deliberate humiliation.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents the project. Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal details.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect confidentiality as practical, and may edit or remove contributions, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove a participant. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and is intentionally summarized for this project. Questions about enforcement may be raised through the same private contact channel.
